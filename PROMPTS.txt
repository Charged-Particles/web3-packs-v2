I need to integrate Axelar/Squid into our Contracts, such that we can accept payment from any supported blockchain.

The steps would be:

1. Pay in USDC on Chain A
2. Use Squid Router to Bridge to Chain B
3. Use Squid Router to convert USDC to WETH for payment
4. Use Axelar to call Web3PacksV2.bundle() on Chain B
5. Wait for Confirmation on Chain A.

This could also happen in reverse; Pay on Chain B, Bundle on Chain A...

Also, we should be able to support more currencies than just USDC, and convert to WETH when bridging.
The file in Context (GEMINI.md) contains some docs/notes on the Axelar Chain and the Squid Router.

Phase 1: Smart Contract Development (On Destination Chain - Mode, Ink, or Polygon)
Create Web3PackReceiver.sol:

This new contract will be responsible for receiving cross-chain messages via Axelar.
It will inherit from AxelarExecutable (from Axelar's GMP SDK).
Its constructor will take the Axelar Gateway address and the address of your deployed Web3PacksV2 contract.
It will implement the _execute(string calldata sourceChain, string calldata sourceAddress, bytes calldata payload) function, which is the entry point for Axelar messages.
It will include a mechanism (e.g., a mapping(address => bool) public trustedSenders;) to whitelist addresses that are allowed to call _execute. This will primarily be the Squid Router's contract address on the destination chain.
Implement _execute Logic in Web3PackReceiver.sol:

Inside _execute, we will first validate the sourceAddress against our trustedSenders whitelist.
We will then define a clear abi.decode structure for the payload that will contain all the necessary parameters to call your Web3PacksV2.bundle() function (e.g., bundleChunks, referrals, tokenMetaUri, lockState, packType, ethPackPrice, and the receiver address).
The ethPackPrice will be the amount of WETH received on the destination chain.
The Web3PackReceiver contract will need to receive the WETH from Squid and then pass it along to Web3PacksV2.bundle(). This might involve IWETH(wethAddress).deposit{value: amount}() if the payment is in native ETH, or IERC20(wethAddress).transfer() if it's already WETH.
Finally, it will call Web3PacksV2(web3PacksV2Address).bundle(...) with the decoded parameters.
Phase 2: Frontend/Off-chain Integration (Using Squid SDK)
Initialize Squid SDK:

Set up your environment with the necessary RPC endpoints, private keys (for the sender), and your Squid integratorId.
Construct the getRoute Call:

Use squid.getRoute() to define the cross-chain swap and call.
fromChain, toChain, fromToken (e.g., USDC on Chain A), toToken (WETH on Chain B), fromAmount, fromAddress, toAddress (this will be the Web3PackReceiver contract address on Chain B).
Crucially, we will use the postHook parameter to specify the call to your Web3PackReceiver contract.
The postHook will include:
target: The address of your deployed Web3PackReceiver.sol contract on Chain B.
callData: The ABI-encoded call to the _execute function of your Web3PackReceiver contract. This callData will contain the sourceChain, sourceAddress, and the payload (which itself contains the bundle() parameters).
value: The amount of WETH that Squid will send to your Web3PackReceiver contract. This will be the ethPackPrice for your bundle() call.
Execute the Route:

Once the route is obtained, use squid.executeRoute() to send the transaction.
Monitor Transaction Status:

Use squid.getStatus() to track the progress of the cross-chain transaction and provide feedback to the user.
